# S07 — Discover sources and decisions

- **When:** Week 03, Monday
- **Skills:** ING-01, OUT-01
- **Prerequisites:** S01
- **Status:** Not started

## Desired outcomes

1. Trace one decision from consumer and action back to source systems, grains, owners, and latency.
2. Draft a data contract covering semantics, keys, freshness, volume, schema change, deletes, sensitivity, and quality expectations.
3. State what the data can describe and what it cannot establish about outcomes or impact.

## Observable evidence

- A source inventory and contract with owners, assumptions, example records, and consumer expectations.
- A decision-to-data trace with explicit unknowns and unsupported claims.

## Optional preparation

See [week 3 reading](../READING.md#week-03), including data contracts and DataOps feedback.

## Stopping point

Stop at one useful source-to-decision slice; avoid cataloguing every hypothetical source.
