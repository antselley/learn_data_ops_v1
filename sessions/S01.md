- **When:** Week 01, Monday
- **Skills:** DEV-01, ARC-01, ING-01, OUT-01
- **Prerequisites:** None

## Desired outcomes

1. Describe one mission decision, its user, action, latency, and the difference between service activity, outcome, prediction, and causal impact.
2. Produce a bounded platform brief covering freshness, volume, concurrency, sensitivity, recovery, supported reporting/serving paths, and cost/maintenance constraints.
3. Explain where Terraform belongs in the lifecycle and what must exist before Terraform can use remote state.

## Observable evidence

- A versioned one-page platform brief with explicit assumptions and non-goals.
- A simple context diagram and a short explanation of the Terraform bootstrap problem.
