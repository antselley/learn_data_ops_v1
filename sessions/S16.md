# S16 — Experiments and causal limits

- **When:** Week 06, Monday
- **Skills:** OUT-02, OUT-05
- **Prerequisites:** S13, S14, S15
- **Status:** Not started

## Desired outcomes

1. Turn one change into a hypothesis, treatment, comparison, assignment mechanism, outcome window, estimand, and guardrails.
2. Distinguish a randomised experiment from observational evaluation and from a predictive model.
3. Explain what missing outcomes, contamination, power, sample size, or equity subgroups prevent the evaluation from concluding.

## Observable evidence

- A one-page evaluation design with assumptions and analysis intent.
- A feasibility note that can recommend “do not run” or a smaller learning objective.

## Optional preparation

See [week 6 reading](../READING.md#week-06) for experimentation and causal-inference foundations.

## Stopping point

Stop once design and feasibility can be challenged; do not simulate certainty through a detailed analysis plan.

## Reflection

_What would change the decision even if statistical significance were unavailable?_
