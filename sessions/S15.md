# S15 — Capacity and unit economics

- **When:** Week 05, Friday
- **Skills:** OUT-04
- **Prerequisites:** S13
- **Status:** Not started

## Desired outcomes

1. Connect demand, arrivals, work-in-progress, throughput, wait time, capacity, and a service outcome.
2. Define a unit-cost view that separates fixed, variable, infrastructure, licence, and maintenance effort.
3. Use the model to frame one resource-allocation decision without treating cost reduction as the mission objective.

## Observable evidence

- A capacity/flow model with measurable assumptions and bottlenecks.
- A unit-economics table and a decision memo describing mission and equity trade-offs.
