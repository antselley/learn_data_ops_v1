# S20 — Spark plans and bottlenecks

- **When:** Week 07, Wednesday
- **Skills:** ARC-04
- **Prerequisites:** S19
- **Status:** Not started

## Desired outcomes

1. Inspect logical and physical plans and connect stages/tasks in the Spark UI to the code.
2. Investigate a shuffle, data skew, partition-sizing problem, or poor join strategy using evidence rather than a guessed tuning rule.
3. Change one factor and explain both its improvement and its limits.

## Observable evidence

- Saved plan/UI observations with a bottleneck hypothesis and confirming evidence.
- Before/after runtime or task-distribution measurements and an explanation of the chosen join/partition strategy.

## Optional preparation

See [week 7 reading](../READING.md#week-07), particularly Spark SQL performance tuning and web UI guidance.

## Stopping point

Stop after diagnosing and testing one meaningful bottleneck; a tuning checklist is not the objective.
