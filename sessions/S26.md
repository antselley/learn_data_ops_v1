- **When:** Week 09, Wednesday
- **Skills:** ML-02
- **Prerequisites:** S25

## Desired outcomes

1. Choose time-aware, entity-aware, or grouped validation that matches how predictions will be made.
2. Define point-in-time-correct feature availability and deliberately identify a leakage path.
3. Compare credible and leaked/naive results, including uncertainty and subgroup/sample-size limits.

## Observable evidence

- A split diagram with entity, time, feature cutoff, label window, and deployment relationship.
- A leakage analysis and comparison showing why the invalid estimate is misleading.
