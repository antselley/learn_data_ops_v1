# S31 — Forecasting with constraints

- **When:** Week 11, Monday
- **Skills:** OUT-04, ML-01, ML-02
- **Prerequisites:** S15, S25, S26
- **Status:** Not started

## Desired outcomes

1. Frame a forecast around a capacity or resource-allocation action, horizon, update cadence, and decision lead time.
2. Compare against simple seasonal/naive baselines using rolling-origin validation and action-relevant errors.
3. Communicate uncertainty, shocks, missing data, and where the sample cannot support granular forecasts.

## Observable evidence

- A forecast decision contract and time-aware backtest design.
- Baseline/candidate evidence translated into a capacity choice, including uncertainty and “do not use” conditions.

## Optional preparation

See [week 11 reading](../READING.md#week-11) for time-series evaluation and capacity links.

## Stopping point

Stop after one horizon and aggregation level; complex forecasting methods wait until a baseline fails usefully.

## Reflection

_Does the forecast improve the decision beyond a simple buffer or seasonal rule?_
