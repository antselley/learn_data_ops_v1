## Desired outcomes

1. Independently diagnose an introduced failed model release, such as lineage mismatch, leakage, scoring skew, corrupt publication, or performance regression.
2. Roll back predictions/model safely and reproduce the last trusted evaluation from recorded inputs.
3. Define evidence-based investigation, retraining, rollback, and retirement criteria rather than automatic retraining on any drift.

## Observable evidence

- An incident timeline with hypotheses, evidence, root cause, affected outputs, and containment.
- Rollback/reconciliation proof plus a runbook specifying retrain and retirement triggers.
