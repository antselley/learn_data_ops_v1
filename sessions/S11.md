## Desired outcomes

1. Express an equivalent small model graph in plain SQL workflow terms and dbt workflow terms.
2. Compare testing, documentation, dependency management, environments, collaboration, debugging, and dependency cost.
3. Decide when dbt earns its complexity for the target organisation and when plain SQL remains adequate.

## Observable evidence

- A side-by-side workflow/artefact comparison grounded in the same transformation.
- A short architecture decision with adoption and exit criteria.
