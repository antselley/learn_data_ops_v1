# S25 — Baselines before models

- **When:** Week 09, Monday
- **Skills:** ML-01, ML-02
- **Prerequisites:** S13
- **Status:** Not started

## Desired outcomes

1. Frame one regression or classification task from the action a prediction would support, including prediction time, eligible population, horizon, and consequence of errors.
2. Establish a simple policy, prevalence, or statistical baseline before a more complex model.
3. Choose evaluation metrics and thresholds from operational value, capacity, equity, and error costs.

## Observable evidence

- A prediction specification that distinguishes predicted outcome from causal effect.
- Baseline and candidate evaluation with a decision-linked metric/threshold rationale.

See [week 9 reading](../READING.md#week-09) for estimator and evaluation guidance.

## Stopping point

Stop after one honest baseline comparison; model breadth and hyperparameter search are out of scope.
