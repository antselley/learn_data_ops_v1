# S18 — Promote, recover, and cost

- **When:** Week 06, Friday
- **Skills:** DEV-04, DEV-05, ARC-03
- **Prerequisites:** S05, S09, S17
- **Status:** Not started

## Desired outcomes

1. Rehearse or table-top a versioned promotion through environments with checks, approvals, and safe data publication.
2. Introduce a deployment or batch-publication fault and choose resume, roll forward, or rollback from observable evidence.
3. Compare monthly infrastructure, licensing, engineering maintenance, and recovery effort under normal and growth scenarios.

## Observable evidence

- A promotion/recovery transcript or timeline with a clearly identified released artefact.
- A cost model with assumptions, sensitivity, ownership, and a “complexity justified when” threshold.

See [week 6 reading](../READING.md#week-06), including low-risk releases and Azure cost guidance.

## Stopping point

Stop after one promotion and recovery path plus a decision-grade cost comparison; refine estimates later with measured usage.
