# S09 — Partial-failure recovery lab

- **When:** Week 03, Friday
- **Skills:** DEV-05, ING-02, ING-05
- **Prerequisites:** S08
- **Status:** Not started

## Desired outcomes

1. Diagnose an intentionally introduced failure between extraction, transformation, and safe publication.
2. Recover using idempotent replay without duplicating, omitting, or prematurely exposing records.
3. Reconcile source, landing, and published counts or control totals and explain the remaining uncertainty.

## Observable evidence

- A failure timeline containing symptom, hypotheses, observations, root cause, and recovery action.
- Before/after reconciliation evidence and a tested publication invariant.

## Optional preparation

See [week 3 reading](../READING.md#week-03); focus on feedback and recovery rather than adding tools.

## Stopping point

Stop after recovery and reconciliation of one failure. Record a second failure mode for a future repeat.

_Which signal shortened diagnosis, and which missing signal created guesswork?_
