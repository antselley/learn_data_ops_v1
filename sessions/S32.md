# S32 — End-to-end platform design

- **When:** Week 11, Wednesday
- **Skills:** ARC-01, ARC-03, ARC-05, ING-01, ING-03, OUT-01, OUT-04
- **Prerequisites:** S17, S22, S23, S31
- **Status:** Not started

## Desired outcomes

1. Produce a thin end-to-end design from mission decision through source, ingestion, storage, compute, transformation, reporting/prediction serving, and feedback.
2. Show identity, networking, environments, observability, recovery, persistent-resource protection, and supported consumer paths.
3. Compare at least two architectures on infrastructure/licensing cost, maintenance effort, developer experience, scale threshold, portability, and explicit non-goals.

## Observable evidence

- An architecture pack: context/container diagrams, requirements trace, operational ownership, and recovery/teardown notes.
- A costed decision record with assumptions, alternative, risks, and a trigger for revisiting the choice.
