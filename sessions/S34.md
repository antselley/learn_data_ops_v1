# S34 — Evaluation synthesis

- **When:** Week 12, Monday
- **Skills:** OUT-02, OUT-03, OUT-05, ML-01, ML-02
- **Prerequisites:** S16, S26, S31
- **Status:** Not started

## Desired outcomes

1. For one mission question, choose among descriptive reporting, segmentation, prediction, randomised experiment, or observational causal evaluation—and reject unsuitable approaches.
2. Align population, denominator, outcome, horizon, validation/identification assumptions, equity considerations, and action.
3. State when missing outcomes, selection, leakage, sample size, or operational constraints mean no useful conclusion is currently possible.

## Observable evidence

- A method-selection decision tree applied to the chosen question.
- A concise analysis brief with supported claims, non-claims, and the next information worth collecting.

## Optional preparation

See [week 12 reading](../READING.md#week-12); prioritise retrieval and synthesis over new material.

## Stopping point

Stop at a defensible method and evidence limit; no analysis is preferable to an invalid one.

## Reflection

_Which method was most tempting but least able to answer the actual question?_
