# S14 — Funnels, cohorts, and segments

- **When:** Week 05, Wednesday
- **Skills:** OUT-03
- **Prerequisites:** S13
- **Status:** Not started

## Desired outcomes

1. Define a service or engagement funnel with unambiguous entry, steps, exits, windows, and eligibility.
2. Define cohorts and retention/engagement measures that respect time and changing eligibility.
3. Propose useful segments while flagging multiplicity, unstable small groups, and descriptive-versus-causal limits.

## Observable evidence

- A funnel/cohort specification with example edge cases.
- A segmentation note stating decision value, minimum reporting rules, and claims that are not supported.
