# S13 — Reach, outcomes, and equity

- **When:** Week 05, Monday
- **Skills:** OUT-01, OUT-02
- **Prerequisites:** S07
- **Status:** Not started

## Desired outcomes

1. Separate service activity, reach, intermediate outcomes, longer-term outcomes, prediction, and causal impact for one mission pathway.
2. Define numerators, eligible denominators, observation windows, missing-outcome states, and equity slices.
3. Identify where selection, access, follow-up, or small samples make a conclusion unreliable.

## Observable evidence

- A metric tree or logic model connected to decisions and actions.
- Metric definitions with denominators, missingness, segmentation safeguards, and explicit non-claims.
